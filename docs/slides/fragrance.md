# Fragrance confirms the Netherlands, as long as its market is not overstated

- **The Netherlands still ranks first when fragrance is scored** (78.1 vs Ireland 71.2, +6.8 points, first in all 6 scenarios). It is the biggest fragrance market of the candidates: €457 M in 2025, 2.9 times Ireland's.
- **The Dutch figure is an upper bound.** Raw imports (€1.9 bn) mostly leave again through Rotterdam (76 % re-exported). Rough estimate: if the true level were about 40 % lower, Ireland would take first place (method: Dutch market and per-head figures lowered together, growth unchanged, scoring re-run).
- **No price edge, less crowded than France.** The same perfume costs €1.38 per ml online in every country, so the margin comes from VAT and mix, not shelf price. Perfumeries per 100k inhabitants: 4.4 within 10 km of the 5 largest cities (France 9.4) and 2.1 nationwide (France 2.5). ICI PARIS XL leads.

**Source:** Eurostat Comext DS-045409 (HS 3303, imports − exports, customs values, not retail prices); LOOKFANTASTIC (online prices, 2026-11-12); OpenStreetMap (perfumeries, 2026-11-13, share of shop count). Scenario weights: `config.py` (assumption). France is a net exporter and can't be measured with trade data.
